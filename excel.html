E-Commerce Business Performance Dashboard

I built an interactive Excel dashboard to evaluate sales, profitability, and warehouse efficiency using a fictional e-commerce dataset.

Analytical approach

I used PivotTables to compare revenue across regions and summarize sales and profit by month. I compared totals with per-sale averages to see how the rankings changed with the metric.

Key findings

  • A 24.4× gap in regional revenue. Central recorded the highest total revenue (33,112), and the Caribbean recorded the lowest (1,358). Central Asia led in average sales per transaction (159.53), ahead of Central (139.48).
  • The strongest month depended on the metric. July led in total sales (5,243) and total profit (2,469.45). November led in average profit per sale (81.37).
